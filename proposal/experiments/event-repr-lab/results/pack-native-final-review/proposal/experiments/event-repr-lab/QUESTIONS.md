# Questions to settle with evidence

The semantic contract comes first: all candidates describe the same sets. This
laboratory reopens representation choices, including decisions previously called
the chosen BDD baseline. The priority is native dependency-language synergy,
algebraic elegance, then performance. Performance decides within the workloads and operations
actually measured. A faster computation of a weaker answer is a different lane.

## Decisions this investigation can actually make

The [count specialization](FACTOR-COUNTS.md) answers one more concrete question:
a supported membership FD can eliminate unused legal-product state faces from
observation without changing resident Events. Four Lean reports, 61-coordinate
checks and 110 native processes support that rule; 48 matched count-mode pairs
preserve all resident counts and bytes. The full comparison has 216 configurations
and 552 matched comparisons. Its cutoff repeat favors prefix512 over prefix64
for fresh queries and retained bytes on the larger fixture, while prefix64 has
a faster grouped-layout constructor. This does not solve general correlated-law
contraction or variable-fibre counting. The partial-information workload now
has its own native measurements and exact observation-FD laws.

The [report](REPORT.md) and [raw-table generator](analyze.py) give the measured
answer for each workload and cache state. The result is a selection among exact
implementations of the same operations, not permission to weaken the algebra.

- **BDD identity is not mandatory.** Every finite candidate establishes exact
  resident equality; no conversion through a BDD is needed for the lab queries.
- **Two stored roots are not mandatory for one-bit complement.** The
  [anchored construction](ANCHOR.md) gives an explicit counterexample to that
  earlier design assumption. Its projection costs remain measurable.
- **The query's internal operation mix matters.** Boolean grouping and modal
  composition have different winners. The physical carrier should not be
  selected from the label “probability” or “relational” alone.
- **Caching is part of the representation experiment.** All carriers must get
  the same outer memo opportunity. A DD's internal Apply cache is separately
  intrinsic to its current implementation and is never described as disabled.
- **A fixed symbolic order can fail before arithmetic starts.** The equality
  family changes from a small graph to an explicit node-cap refusal solely by
  changing coordinate order. Increasing a cap is not a solution to that growth.
- **Packed terminals deserve a real place in the comparison.** The repeated
  modal experiment gives them a lead on structured elimination. Their Boolean
  Coup performance remains behind the dispatched dense carrier. The full
  coordinate-movement lane now confirms a leading interleaved packed64/packed512
  group. A same-binary repeat establishes the large gain from table-preserving
  maps; the small gap between those two leaf sizes is not a universal ranking.
- **Smallest graph and fastest query differ.** Block64 retains about 0.22 MB
  on the interleaved full relation query and about 15 KB on symbolic twenty-pair
  equality, but loses to packed terminals on the relational query and to dense
  on compact Coup. Partial abstraction and temporary-buffer kernels need their
  own comparisons before dismissing selector partitions.
- **No automatic adaptive backend is selected here.** The prototypes use one
  manager per scope. A mixed backend must include conversion, identity and
  observation costs in a new matched benchmark before claiming to combine wins.

## What remains, with concrete next experiments

The [native Pack experiment](FACTORIZED-PACK.md) resolves whether the Event
algebra can remove whole families of scalar bindings before Free Join emits
them: yes, for a certified Cartesian branch relation. All 5,376 differential
runs pass; the main and crossover sweeps retain 192 timing configurations.
The twelve separate Lean reports establish denotational rewrites and the
participating-validation boundary. The remaining questions are concrete:
derive separators from normalized queries rather than a fixed constructor;
measure skew and unmatched branches; implement typed relational Pack while
preserving shared worlds and legal support; and cost both schedules. Fanout-one
and small warm regressions already rule out an unconditional switch.

The [information-readout workload](PREFIX-READOUTS.md) now constructs Possible,
Guaranteed and Ambiguous Events after actual Free Join. Suffix projections expose
the stronger prefix certificate; non-suffix controls expose expensive completion
fallbacks. Fourteen additional Lean reports give exact FD conditions for filter
movement and the order between observations. The next measured kernel should
fuse projection with decoder substitution while preserving the entire joint
witness. Canonical reimport needs its own normalization control; its verification
cap must not be mistaken for a timed-query cap. The equal-cofactor control is
now complete: it preserves all twenty paired resident statistics and all four
cap cases under both policies. Direct ITE now completes all four former cap cases and improves the difficult
query 4.35-fold. Its 708,405 retained records still surround only 343 records
reachable from the 64 answers. The completed support-gate round now reduces that query from 392.599 to
10.758 ms with fixed ITE, retaining 35,380 records; packed still takes 1.467 ms.


[A further Lean derivation](PROJECTION-GATES.md) proves when a membership FD
allows an omitted face's support gate to disappear during projection. The
condition is exact joint witness coverage; retained-environment product support
is a sufficient certificate. Coupled support and hidden environments have
checked counterexamples. The separate Rust gate kernel now passes 74 comparison processes, five shared
suites and 32 ownership/symbolic acceptance processes. [Local completion](LOCAL-COMPLETION.md) now passes four shared suites and 110
comparative processes. The difficult query falls from 10.659 to 2.247 ms, while
packed takes 1.587 ms. Locality and source traversal contribute independently.
The completed [direct-construction comparison](FUSED-PROJECTION.md) combines source
abstraction and target substitution while retaining one shared witness and a
signed source-root cache key. Eight axiom-free step laws now pass in Lean, and the Rust
control passes five differential suites. All 81 comparative processes and
sixteen acceptance processes pass. Three-sample readout medians favor staging:
2.229 versus 2.711 ms in bit-major order, and 2.964 versus 3.286 ms in face-major
order. The canonical answer graphs are identical; fusion is an available exact
schedule, not the preferred schedule for this family.

The [storage-closure review](../../event-storage.md) resolves another semantic
restriction: empty belongs to stored Event fields. Ten new Lean reports prove
the dependency consequences, including the failure of complete-key uniqueness
without nonemptiness. Scalar constraints still see empty-valued facts. Native
persistence and planner integration must test that distinction explicitly.

The remaining questions fall into distinct implementation work rather than a
single missing carrier decision:

- **Coordinate capabilities:** masks now cover 61 coordinates in the constrained
  symbolic fixture; the current legal constructor requires fewer than 63. Local permutations validate bijectivity, rebuild canonical
  order and keep MDD padding fixed. Arbitrary coordinate sets, exact counts beyond
  `u64`, general source/environment alignment and guard substitution remain open.
  Finite binary cross-space support embeddings now pass the transport checks;
  this is narrower than the production CoordinateMap contract.
- **Decomposition under relational maps:** the first three-layout comparison,
  larger leaf cuts and selector-block implementation are now measured. The
  [same-binary map control](TAIL-MAPS.md) separates a table kernel defect from
  the representation. The [essential-coordinate table design](ESSENTIAL.md) specifies a new
  canonical competitor whose local cut follows exact Boolean dependence rather
  than a fixed tail. Both complete scoped carriers now pass the shared suite and
  [163 native cases](ESSENTIAL-BASELINE.md); the first timings favor the existing
  fixed-tail controls. A derived algorithm path removes redundant normalization,
  shares complemented operation memos and caches exact structural counts. It
  passes all 48 native sweep/repeat processes, improving both carriers while
  leaving fixed-tail controls ahead. The [compact-slab experiment](SLABS.md)
  now implements single-copy payloads and complete collision checks. Its 146
  matched case pairs retain the same logical counts: carrier bytes fall while
  latency is mixed, with a repeated scalar-classifier regression. The
  [borrowed-word classifier](WORD-CLASSIFIER.md) now contracts local tables in
  the same traversal: 144 matched pairs preserve resident storage and exact
  results. Independent repeats confirm large gains, including ordered cuts
  where some cells must be proved empty. Fixed-tail/dense controls still lead
  several fresh queries. [Bounded scratch](SCRATCH.md) now supplies a separate
  alignment-buffer control; clone elimination remains another experiment. Variable-width
  blocks, dense versus selector branch payloads,
  repeated renamed templates and conversions remain to test. The
  [decomposition review](../../research/block-decomposition.md) and earlier
  [representation review](../../research/representation-search.md) record the
  relevant theorem limits. General AOMDD canonicality requires an accepted
  pseudo-tree; a complement can destroy a product decomposition.
- **Query-specific fixed points:** compare materialized `R*` then May with
  backward reachable-state iteration, frontier iteration, and eventually a
  recursive Reach kernel, returning the same state event. Test both bounded
  components and a long counter chain, plus the paper's alternating-leading-bit
  counterexample. The independent schedule check confirms that the last family
  removes the recursive counter advantage; retain it in native acceptance.
  The existing full relation lane must
  retain its closure, converse and residual outputs; a state-only answer cannot
  replace it in the leaderboard. The retained Reach paper motivates this
  separate lane and shows why naive iteration can itself be exponentially worse.
  The [diagonal experiment](DIAGONAL.md) now removes the relation program's
  explicit identity-bitplane construction while retaining direct finite controls.
  Nine carriers pass sixty-coordinate identity/composition/residual checks without
  world enumeration. A separate native long-chain fixture passes 27 cases across nine carriers,
  including million-state full closure and four other outputs. The matched
  identity sweep passes 246 owned-query cases across all fifteen carriers.
  Neither lane implements query-specific recursive Reach; the independent
  backward-block checker now verifies its equations in 393,304 finite cases.
- **Renamed operand views:** the [kernel](VIEW-PRODUCT.md) passes raw/shared
  checks and 78 native processes. Its 96 matched strategy pairs reject blanket
  replacement of canonical intermediates: saved nodes can cost time, and fused
  output renaming can increase partial outputs. The [factorial control](VIEW-ORDER.md)
  passes 292 native processes and 768 matched comparisons. Delayed output
  renaming can strongly reduce retained nodes, with mixed speed outcomes.
  The isolated product replay enters no branch fallback: repeated alignment
  and loss of normalized residual sharing are separate hypotheses. The
  [normalization/reuse control](REUSE.md) now implements both with exact caches,
  raw/shared checks and an untimed 192-case diagnostic. Its 912 native query
  cases and nine-sample repeats show that canonical cofactors can recover much
  of the lost speed, while plane reuse is a smaller independent gain with some
  overhead regressions. Source normalization retains more nodes; output-late
  execution can still matter. Packed and dense remain competitive controls. Canonical publication, support admission and complete root/map memo
  identity remain fixed. The public Event is never a pending execution view.
- **Legal domains and dependency views:** a Cartesian product of legal
  categorical domains need not fill its bit encoding's cube. The
  [scoped adapter](SCOPED-PRODUCT.md) now retains arbitrary support with exact
  witness/output gates. The stronger relation-program constructor now verifies
  an environment-indexed legal-domain product by containment and exact count. The
  [supported-dependence analysis](SUPPORTED-DEPENDENCE.md) separates raw masks
  from logical functional dependencies: on x=y, either x or y determines the
  event, but neither is a unique least choice. The [legal-relation review](LEGAL-RELATIONS.md)
  separates product support from the membership dependency on declared faces;
  even full support cannot turn an arbitrary three-face Event into a binary
  relation. Native checked roles, symbolic legal-domain construction and exact
  support-map certificates now pass the shared checks. The next boundary is
  preserving those capabilities through production cross-owner publication and
  planner readouts. Use exact joint images for more general maps; no probability
  independence follows from product support.
- **Observation caching:** current counts rescan finite containers or traverse
  diagrams with a fresh memo. Compare stored cardinalities against that baseline,
  including construction cost and retained bytes. A cached uniform count is
  still not a joint-law contraction algorithm.
  The essential `derived` path now stores exact counts over each node's essential
  axes and smooths unused ambient axes. Its bundled same-binary improvement is
  measured; an isolated count-cache toggle is still needed to attribute the
  gain to cardinality storage alone.

- **Direct relationship classification:** the [kernel brief](CLASSIFY.md)
  specifies three independent paths:
  construct the four canonical Venn regions then test emptiness; traverse the
  support and two operands together to accumulate occupied cells with early
  termination; and reuse cached signatures. Keep the sixteen-byte keep-table
  filter separate from classification cost, as the actual Allen kernel does.
  Partial support must participate in the traversal: the raw all-false cell
  includes impossible outside-support assignments unless explicitly restricted.
  Complement/swap act on the four signature bits after checked scope alignment.
  A read-only test need not publish four intermediate Events, but a requested
  counterexample Event must still pay construction/publication. Charge cache
  bytes, repeated versus novel pairs, and actual native filter selectivity.
  The allocated-word control is now measured under both essential stores.
  The [bounded-scratch control](SCRATCH.md) now replaces only its alignment
  buffers, preserving scalar and allocated-word paths. The relationship histogram stays complete; predicate-specific early
  termination requires a separately labelled answer contract.

- **Source/guard solver:** Boolean diagrams do not decide arbitrary real
  semialgebraic feasibility. The chosen source constructors still need exact
  satisfiability, domain, and normalization certificates. This lab fixes finite
  admissibility and never treats a model probability as proof of feasibility.
- **Joint-law contraction:** the [exact observation experiment](OBSERVATION.md)
  now implements shared conditional-iid groups, exact Beta integration, rational
  point laws and explicit zero evidence in all fifteen carriers. It preserves
  joint dependence before integration and exercises the full native relation
  query. General correlated factors, semialgebraic guards and certified bounds
  remain separate work; this coefficient representation does not solve them.
- **Persistent identity:** resident canonicality and wire canonicality are
  different. The [transport experiment](TRANSPORT.md) now rebases between
  carriers and orders, checks allocation-independent exports, and compares
  common packed512 destination bytes with independently constructed bytes.
  The real engine still lacks persisted Event scalar values and source/law
  descriptors. Verify identical individual facts after reopen, independently
  of surrounding packet roots. Charge conversion and object resolution.
  A further packet experiment can remove irrelevant axes from individual
  tables while retaining the complete ambient coordinate roster and source law.
  Compare smaller packets with the cost of rebuilding arbitrary local tables;
  absence from a predicate's table must not delete a draw from its law.
  Support-proof construction is currently fresh per restoration and uses the
  source carrier. Compare a retained checked map, a common proof carrier, and
  a proved full-product constant case before treating that setup as intrinsic
  conversion cost. Malformed coordinates/supports must still fail first.
- **Incremental FD/IND admission:** a two-Event coverage/conflict summary and
  balanced update tree now pass exact mixed-update checks across all carriers;
  [the dependency review](DEPENDENCIES.md) records its algebra and bridge to
  the native interval judge. A passing finite bridge cannot choose persistent
  update indexing, establish native Event admission, or benchmark long-lived
  transactions. Those remain distinct acceptance steps.
- **Ownership and reclamation:** retained batches now keep scopes alive across
  restoration and native replay; invalid owner/unpublished IDs are rejected.
  Within-scope intermediates still accumulate. Native spill, cancellation,
  concurrent publication and compaction require their own tests; the trusted
  lab publishing boundary is not a finished public constructor.
  The earlier transport replay cloned a carrier with imported expected outputs.
  The [owned lane](OWNED.md) now imports only inputs, computes through a mutable
  borrow of the actual owner and publishes novel results. All fifteen carriers
  pass 82 finite native cases, including final-owner lifetime and reclamation.
  Transaction rollback and cancellation remain outside that trusted lab boundary. One-bit carriers now publish both
  polarities together; the checker resolves the complement before publishing
  an inverse batch and verifies involution without graph allocation.
- **Information-state sufficiency:** finite closure needs a finite stable state
  quotient. The schema does not establish which finite memory suffices for all
  Coup strategies. A bounded rule/information arena can be benchmarked before
  making any broader claim.
- **Inference quality:** Noul/Choice/Score forecasts and their coupling/import
  assumptions need observed outcomes. Exact algebra preserves a forecast's
  consequences; representation benchmarks cannot assess its calibration.

The retained primary-source reading already answers the algebraic distinctions:
KAD supplies constructive preconditions, knowledge compilation explains operation
and ordering tradeoffs, FAQ constrains elimination order, and Jeffrey/Pearl updates
separate posterior targets from likelihoods. [The adjudication](../../research/algebra-resolution.md)
records those scopes. This round tests implementation choices against the actual
engine rather than treating those papers as benchmark results.


“Effect algebra” is now explicitly grounded in Definition 15 of Cho et al.:
Event partitions form sharp tests under disjoint union. The full relational
operators retain their own concrete semantics. Generic numeric/quantum effect
algebras do not automatically supply the proposal's Boolean meet, converse,
residuals or reachability. This terminology question is resolved; representation
and exact-law implementation questions remain measurable work.
