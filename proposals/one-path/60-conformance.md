# 60 — Conformance under one path

Tests die only when their requirement died; every deletion below names the
dead requirement. Tests reshape where the requirement survives in a new
form. Two lanes are born. The battery at the end must be green WHOLE.

## Dies with its requirement

- **F3's quantitative W cells** (slack/slack+1 boundaries, evaporation
  fixtures, the naive point-delta refutation, weighted/unit/parent± rows):
  the interval law is deleted; there is no arithmetic to keep honest.
- **F3's commute-cell no-re-judgment pins** and the re-judgment-counter-
  at-zero assertions: the fast arms are deleted; nothing must stay at
  zero.
- **F5's lying-winner fixture**: no carried section exists to lie in.
- **F5's survive-vs-fork marker-file proofs and both engine-decided
  subsumption arms**: the arms are deleted.
- **F5/lane_e's disjoint-never-re-judges pins and re-addressed-header
  checks**: republish is deleted.
- **f9's footprint-comparator storm** (mutated sections must land
  FootprintMismatch): the section and the refusal are deleted. The batch
  decoder and manifest/checkpoint/sidecar parser storms stay whole.
- **lane_a footprint/intersect suites, the footprint alloc windows, the
  corpus's footprint goldens and sidecar expectations, the parity
  footprint sections (Rust and TS)**: subjects deleted. The corpus
  regenerates as header+ops; bless-style, both languages re-pinned.
- **F2's one-braid disjoint-pair oracle**: its filter was
  `footprint+intersect`; with the predicate deleted, the fixture class is
  unstatable. The multi-braid half survives (below).

## Reshapes

- **F2 → braid convergence**: random multi-braid histories under seeded
  interleavings all converge to one `catalog_digest` and one generation —
  L9's executable shadow, now the lane's whole content (the order-quotient
  digest makes it sound for string-free and string-carrying fixtures
  alike; keep the recorded string-interleaving scope note).
- **F3 → the serial-verdict lane**: for each statement family, a
  hand-fixture racing pair on a shared base where the loser's re-judgment
  must produce exactly the serial verdict — the double-booking FD
  rejection, the dangling-reference verdict per order, the capacity
  ceiling/floor rejections, the reservation spend/reclaim races, and the
  byte-equal absorption case. Small, total over the families, named by
  scenario instead of dead matrix coordinates.
- **F5 rewrites to one path**: fleets of 2/4/8 writers, mostly-disjoint
  and adversarial mixes; gates keep everything structural — gap-free
  chains, each slot created once, every prev verified, every acked commit
  exactly once, convergence by digest, wholeness identity after every
  fixture, ambiguity-drop resolution, livelock → `Err::Contention` with
  the violation-sourced HotKey payload and the SlotRace arm, the
  stale-pending writer resolving through re-open with ONE race at tip.
  The Feral storms stay at their recorded parameters (the uniqueness
  storm's round scaling note carries forward), association-storm
  non-vacuity counter added (40).
- **lane_e loser tests rewrite** to the one arm: subsumed-shaped fixtures
  now assert Accepted-at-current-generation via the publish law;
  disjoint-shaped fixtures assert the re-judged republish lands with a
  fresh header at tip+1; conflicting fixtures assert the serial Rejected.
- **F1, F4, F6, F8 stand almost whole** (determinism, crash matrices,
  PITR/gc, engine pins): F4's step enum loses no steps (the discipline
  keeps encode→pending→apply→ack→put→advance→clear); its loser-window
  cells simplify to the one arm; F1 gains the hop-ran assertion (40); F6
  gains nothing and loses nothing; F8 unchanged.
- **Counters**: one remains (losses ≡ re-judgments). Every fixture that
  pinned the dead counters re-pins on structure (chain contents, digests,
  outcomes) instead — counters are instrumentation, structure is truth.
