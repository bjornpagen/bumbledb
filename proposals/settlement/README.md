# settlement — the canon, the endgame, the proof

The one proposals folder. Its predecessors retired the way this repo
retires closed campaigns: `grail/` when the owner closed that pass, the
numbered PRD set (`00-product` … `90-rollout`) and
`representation-first-cutover/` when the cutover landed their substance
in the tree. What survives them lives here, rotated:

- **The law** — product laws, deployment cases, non-goals, and the six
  landed representations, stated as facts of the present tree:
  [00-canon.md](00-canon.md). Normative. The build implements it or
  reports the gap.
- **The remainder** — the finite list between the tree and the canon:
  [10-endgame.md](10-endgame.md). One open representational change
  ([20-one-encoding.md](20-one-encoding.md): JSON dies, `Vector` owns
  its algebra), the reconciliation to green, the proof, the version, the
  receipt.
- **The proof artifacts** — [90-traceability.md](90-traceability.md),
  the 141-finding acceptance checklist the endgame audits adversarially,
  and [RULINGS.md](RULINGS.md), the log of every spelling flexed to keep
  an invariant.
- **The dispatch** — [DISPATCH.md](DISPATCH.md), the orchestrator prompt
  that runs the endgame to the receipt.

When the receipt lands, the canon describes the tree with no remainder,
and this folder is eligible for the same retirement it gave its
predecessors.
